# Code of Conduct

Treat participants with respect. Discuss code and ideas without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers follow the same rules.

Report incidents privately to mattias@yetric.com. Include relevant links and context. Reports will be handled confidentially where possible; do not publish another person's private information in an issue.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, ask participants to change their behavior, or restrict participation for repeated or serious violations. Decisions should be proportionate and explained to the people involved when possible.
